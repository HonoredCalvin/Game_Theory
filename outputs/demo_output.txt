Key insights:
  * P(Attack) = c/L: a higher breach loss L makes the attacker attack LESS often, because the attack rate
    must keep the defender indifferent between monitoring and ignoring.
  * P(Monitor) = g/(g+f) depends ONLY on the attacker's payoffs: a bigger penalty f means LESS monitoring.
  * Regime change at L = c: if monitoring costs at least as much as the loss it prevents, the equilibrium
    is pure (Ignore, Attack).
